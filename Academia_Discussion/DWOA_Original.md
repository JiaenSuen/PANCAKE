# DWOA-original

DWOA-original is the first discrete Whale Optimization implementation retained as an ablation baseline. The continuous WOA concepts of encircling, exploration, and spiral movement are approximated through partial alignment, reversal, block relocation, and source mutation. It obtained **30.84% median RPD**, **0% Success@5%**, **0.94× GA-relative convergence efficiency**, and a **7.37 mean rank**. Its exact gap was **7.44%**, somewhat better than its main-suite rank but still far from the redesigned methods. The main limitation is that the original mapping changes route order and source selection with weak coordination. In PANCAKE, these two decision layers are coupled, so a route move can invalidate a previously useful source decision and vice versa. This baseline became an important diagnostic rather than a failed endpoint: its limitations directly motivated coupled order–source moves, adaptive VNS, diversity monitoring, and stagnation-triggered refinement in DWOA-AVNS.
