# Tabu Search

Tabu Search represents a dedicated local-search baseline. It repeatedly explores route/source neighborhoods while a tabu memory prevents immediate cycling back to recently visited moves. It achieved **13.78% median RPD**, **6.7% Success@5%**, **1.40× GA-relative convergence efficiency**, and a **4.00 mean rank**; the exact-suite median gap was **9.39%**. Its advantage is strongest when the landscape contains a clear sequence of incremental improvements because a concentrated neighborhood scan can exploit such structure efficiently. Its limitation appears when useful progress requires coordinated changes across several order and source decisions. In these cases a one-step neighborhood may temporarily worsen the objective before reaching a better basin. This behavior motivated the later DWOA and DFOX designs: strong local search remains valuable, but exploration mechanisms are needed to move between structurally different basins rather than only intensifying the current one.
