# Discrete Particle Swarm Optimization (DPSO)

The DPSO baseline replaces continuous particle velocity with discrete alignment toward personal and global best solutions, together with route reversal and source-change moves. It reached **26.65% median RPD**, **0% Success@5%**, **0.92× GA-relative convergence efficiency**, and a **7.00 mean rank**; its exact gap was **10.71%**. The weak aggregate result highlights a representation problem: velocity has a clear geometric meaning in a continuous vector space, but only an indirect interpretation in a permutation plus categorical source vector. Leader-following can therefore collapse diversity before useful adjacency and order–source relationships have been preserved. DPSO remains valuable as a negative control because it demonstrates that simply translating swarm terminology into discrete alignment is insufficient. The stronger DWOA-AVNS and DFOX-APNS results support the broader conclusion that the quality of the discrete operator semantics matters more than a literal imitation of the original continuous update equation.
