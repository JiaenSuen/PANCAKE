# Ant Colony Optimization (ACO)

ACO constructs routes through pheromone-guided probabilistic decisions and therefore naturally emphasizes reusable edges and shared locations. It achieved **24.47% median RPD**, **20.0% Success@5%**, and **1.71× GA-relative convergence efficiency**, but its **6.00 mean rank** and **9.91% exact gap** show that rapid early progress did not translate into consistently strong final solutions. The Shared-Hubs landscape is the most favorable case: repeated commodities can exploit common hub structure, and ACO obtained the lowest mean cost on the representative SH-01 map. In contrast, price–distance conflicts and deceptive grouped sources demand coordinated changes that are difficult to express through edge pheromone alone. ACO is therefore a useful example of why convergence speed and terminal solution quality should be measured separately. It can identify strong structural motifs quickly while still settling into a suboptimal basin when source selection and route order interact in more complex ways.
