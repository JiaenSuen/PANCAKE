# GA + Tabu Hybrid

GA+Tabu is the strongest conventional hybrid baseline in this study. GA first supplies population-level recombination and global exploration, while Tabu Search intensifies promising solutions through explicit local neighborhoods. It achieved **11.08% median RPD**, **20.0% Success@5%**, **1.25× GA-relative convergence efficiency**, and a **3.20 mean rank**. On the exact-solvable mini-suite, its **1.69% median exact gap** was close to the best result. The method performed particularly well on the Uniform landscape, showing that a simple exploration-then-intensification pipeline remains difficult to beat when the local neighborhood is informative. This baseline is methodologically important because it prevents the study from attributing every gain to a novel swarm metaphor. DFOX-APNS obtained better aggregate metrics, but the paired comparison with GA+Tabu did not cross the 0.05 significance threshold in this pilot, so the evidence supports competitiveness rather than a universal superiority claim.
