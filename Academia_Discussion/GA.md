# Genetic Algorithm (GA)

GA provides the population-based evolutionary baseline for the study. A chromosome represents both the visit permutation and source choices; recombination transfers partial route/source structure while mutation perturbs order or source assignments. Under the equal 1,200-evaluation budget, GA obtained **28.04% median RPD**, **0% Success@5%**, **1.00× reference convergence efficiency**, and a **7.53 mean rank**. Its exact-suite median gap was **10.58%**. The result indicates that generic crossover maintains useful diversity but lacks sufficiently strong problem-specific intensification for this coupled representation. The route order and source vector interact: a locally attractive source can become poor after the visiting order changes, so independent genetic changes often disrupt good structures. GA therefore serves as an important neutral baseline. Improvements achieved by hybrid or swarm variants can be interpreted as gains from better neighborhood semantics and exploitation rather than merely from maintaining a population.
